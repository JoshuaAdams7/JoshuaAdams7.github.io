Please take a look at my mini portfolio of Azure/365 projects that have of a focus on security, all part of my study towards sitting the Security Operations Analyst Associate (SC-200) exam.

## Outline

* Manage a security operations environment (20–25%)
* Configure protections and detections (15–20%)
* Manage incident response (25–30%)
* Manage security threats (15–20%)

## Why should I attempt to obtain this certification?

The vast majority of the jobs available within cybsersecurity are SOC-based, maning that if you're able to get hands-on with one of the leading security stacks that SOC teams use, you will undoubtedly put yourself ahead of those that don't have the level of exposure that this qualification will give you.

## How difficult is the exam to pass?

Despite having 2 years of experience working with the tools and technologies used as the basis for this exam, and having spent time outside of work hours working with both Sentinel and Defender XDR, I would rate the difficulty of this exam at an 8/10.

* It's highly recommended that you spend time getting hands-on with the technologies being used

## What areas should I pay the most attention to?

* Kusto Query Language (KQL) is a large component in this exam, so make sure that you understand the syntax of queries built using the language.
* Be aware of the process of setting up and configuring Security CoPilot.
* Know your permissions for roles associated with Sentinel, Defender XDR and Security CoPilot.
